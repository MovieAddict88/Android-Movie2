# ChoreQuest KID SAFETY & COPPA CHECKLIST

ChoreQuest is designed to be fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## 1. No Personal Information Collection
- **Kids do not use emails.** They are added to a Family by a parent.
- **No real names required.** Parents can use nicknames for child profiles.
- **No Geolocation.** The app does not request or store GPS data.
- **No Third-Party Tracking.** Standard analytics and crash reporting are disabled for kid-facing screens.

## 2. Parental Consent & Control
- **Verifiable Parental Consent (VPC).** Account creation requires a paid subscription or credit card verification to ensure the user is an adult.
- **AI Opt-in.** AI features are disabled by default. Parents must explicitly enable them in the Dashboard.
- **AI Monitoring.** Every prompt and response is logged in the "AI Safety Logs" for parent review.

## 3. AI Safety (Gemini 2.0 Flash)
- **Hardened Safety Settings.** `BLOCK_LOW_AND_ABOVE` enforced for all categories (Harassment, Hate Speech, Sexually Explicit, Dangerous).
- **Kid-Friendly System Prompts.** AI is instructed to use simple language and avoid scary topics.
- **Manual Review.** Parents can flag and block future similar AI requests.

## 4. Data Deletion
- Parents can delete a child profile at any time, which wipes all associated chores, rewards, and AI logs.
- "Right to be forgotten" is honored through the account deletion feature.

## 5. Screen Time & Limits
- Configurable AI usage limits (default 30 mins/day).
- Automatic lockouts after bedtime (configurable by parent).
